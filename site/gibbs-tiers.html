How Gibbs says to draft it

His words, from the August 14 write-up, not from this board.

QB

Wait. "By far, my favorites have been the ones where I waited as long as possible to select a QB." He is well above consensus on Kyler Murray, Malik Willis, Baker Mayfield and Jared Goff, and calls Josh Allen in Round 4 the green goblin mask.

RB

Leave the first three rounds with at least two backs. Javonte Williams and D'Andre Swift are fine Round 4 security; Cam Skattebo is the Round 4 dice roll. Bucky Irving and Bhayshul Tuten are the Round 5 upside shots. Other than Jonathon Brooks he avoids the position entirely in Rounds 6 through 9, then stacks handcuff-adjacent backs late.

WR

After the top three RBs go, a stack of receivers look like great Round 1 picks. He generally avoids WR in Round 2, with A.J. Brown the exception. Rounds 3 through 6 are where the league winners are, and he still likes plenty of names in the lower-hit-rate 7 to 11 range.

TE

He prefers to wait but will take a mid-round shot sooner than at QB. Sam LaPorta is his mispriced call, Tucker Kraft nearly as much, and Tyler Warren and Harold Fannin Jr. are both valid. Double-dipping a TE with his own WR is fine by him. Late: Dallas Goedert on ADP value, Gunnar Helm and Terrance Ferguson as Year 2 breakouts, Brenton Strange as the free glue guy.
