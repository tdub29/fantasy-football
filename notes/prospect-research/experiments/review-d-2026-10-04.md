# Independent review: QB B12 and the B14 career-stage model, 2026-10-04

Reviewer: a fresh agent (Opus); reproduced B11/B12/B14 exactly and restored the rewritten files.

## Verdict

- **QB B12 for the next class: REJECT as served.** The model's own numbers reproduce and hold up, but
  the board would serve it through the exit-scenario transform, and a backtest of that transform on
  QBs fails (below).
- **B14 for underclassmen (RB/WR/TE): REJECT.** As wired, B14 would only ever score stage-1 rows, and
  stage 1 alone fails the rules at every position; the stage 1-2 pass comes from stage 2.
- **B10 (RB, WR) and B8 (TE) stay for the next class.** On the same final-season rows B14 is worse
  (RB 0.1011 vs 0.0986, WR 0.0649 vs 0.0622, TE 0.1058 vs 0.1004; beats them in 1-3% of resamples).

## Findings

1. **Reproduction: PASS.**
2. **B14 leakage.** Class grouping and feature cutoffs PASS. Weight is the last roster listing; ESPN's
   per-season weight is effectively the same value, and dropping weight moves stage 1-2 log loss by at
   most 0.0021: REVIEW, bounded. Row membership needs the last roster position to be a skill position
   (drops 2.8% of stage-1 qualifying seasons); early dropouts (5.6-8.9% of stage-1 rows, hit rate about
   0) are trained on but never served, so served players run about 20% low at RB stage 1 and TE stage
   1-2: REVIEW. Retrained only on windows closed by each row's season, stage 1-2 still passes, but the
   WR stage-1 gain disappears.
3. **Weighting: PASS.** Equal weight per player still passes stage 1-2 (RB 0.1839 to 0.1656, WR 0.1128
   to 0.0923, TE 0.2203 to 0.1920).
4. **B14 serving: FAIL.** Classes after the next one are stage 1 only. Stage 1 alone: RB 0.2274 to
   0.2244 (62%, slope 0.67), WR 0.1345 to 0.1272 (84%, 2020+ worse), TE 0.2385 to 0.2245 (77%, slope 0.67,
   8 events).
5. **B12 QB.** Class 2020 supplies 49% of the gain; C is effectively 0.3; the slope stays in range in
   82% of class resamples (90% interval 0.68-1.27): REVIEW. **Served transform: FAIL.** Backtest of the
   exact transform (retrained on windows closed by k+1, completed seasons 2011-2020, 663 next-class
   QB-seasons, 25 events): mean 0.0265 vs 0.0377 observed, slope 0.72, first-eligible QBs 0.035 vs
   0.086. Cause: the transform adds the remaining seasons to `n_seasons` and `n_qual` but not to
   `starts` (one start is worth 1.24x the odds); 12 of 99 current next-class QBs have zero completed
   starts, outside the training data. Adding starts fixes the mean but not the slope (0.73).

## Required, and what was done

1. `STAGE` stays empty. Done (unchanged).
2. A B14 retry must be judged on what would be served: stage-1 rows of players still playing, trained
   only on windows closed by each row's season. Not attempted yet.
3. Before QB enters `PREDRAFT`: add the remaining seasons to `starts` for a current starter, return no
   number at zero completed starts, and pass a backtest of the served transform (slope 0.75-1.25,
   calibrated at stage 2). Registered as addendum 8.
