- **Reviewer:** a separate agent (Opus) that did not build the scoring, the display change or the
  monitor, and did not write their briefs. It edited no file except this one.

## Round 2 (same day): sha256, hit line only, no pre-draft numbers, monitor

### What was checked

- Reproduction: re-ran into scratch again. `results`, `sources_sha256` and `script_sha256` match,
  `M4-oof.csv` is byte-identical, and the frozen `p` and `fit` match. The frozen file holds only
  QB12, RB24, WR24 and TE12. The recorded `script_sha256` (8fcbb169...61b6) equals the sha256 of
  the script on disk.
- Callers: `pmTopChance` returns null for any cut other than the hit line, and `pmFitPick` returns a
  pick only for drafted rows. Every caller (`pmMean`, `pmScore`, card hero, two-way line, `pmLadder`,
  board `t1`/`sc`) therefore gets null for first cuts and college rows. No `t0` column remains. One
  leftover is `CF_DIR.t0` (app.js:13676), which is harmless. `pmLadder` still shows first-cut rates,
  but those are observed shares among neighbours, not model output. `node --check` passes.
- Monitor: `--self-check` passes, and the live run exits 0 with "no newly closed class yet" at every
  position. `out` is present only for classes whose window has closed, so partial windows are never scored.

### Defects

1. **m4_monitor.py:61-66, and the kill rule in model-card.md:149-155 and the registry maintenance row.**
   The slope test is a point estimate on one newly closed class (RB 2024 n=17, WR 31). I simulated
   perfectly calibrated forecasts: the kill fired in 52% of runs at n=30, 51% at n=60 and 30% at
   n=150. `slope()` also raised `LinAlgError: Singular matrix` on a separable sample. Charter rule 5
   is applied to roughly 100-300 pooled out-of-fold rows, so this is not "matching charter rule 5".
   Fix: trigger the kill on slope only when a bootstrap interval of the slope lies entirely outside
   0.75-1.25, or require pooled n of at least 150. Keep the 90% hit-interval test (about 10% false
   alarms by design). Guard the Newton solve. Add a calibrated-null false-kill rate to the self-check.
2. **The first-cut claim is wrong for RB12 and TE6.** RB12's K0 slope is 1.2226 and TE6's is 1.03,
   both inside 0.75-1.25. Only QB6 (0.58) and WR12 (0.69) fail. The claim appears at app.js:13591
   and app.js:14014 ("Top-6 QB/TE and top-12 RB/WR odds ... fail the calibration check"),
   model-card.md:137-138, the ledger-m4.md footer and the registry "Not shown" text. Fix: "QB6 and
   WR12 fail the slope band; RB12 and TE6 are hidden because the first cut is not a charter target."

Everything else agrees across code, card, registry and ledger.

## Round 3 (same day): bootstrap slope kill, 95% tests, first-cut wording

**Verdict: PASS.** Both round 2 defects are fixed. Two wording nits remain, neither blocking.

### What was checked

- `m4_monitor.py --self-check` passes. It prints calibrated-null false-kill rates of 9.5% (1 x 30),
  6.0% (2 x 40) and 4.0% (5 x 60), and the overconfident forecast still trips the monitor. The live
  run exits 0 with no newly closed class.
- My own simulation used forecasts from the served WR24 table at picks spread over 10-250, with a
  200-resample bootstrap and 150 runs per cell:
  - Calibrated null: 1.3% kills at 1 class x 30; 12% at 3 x 30. That is near the 10% budget,
    within the simulation's noise (SE about 3 points).
  - Truth half as extreme as the served odds (forecast overconfident by a factor of 2): 29% kills at
    1 x 30 and 84% at 3 x 30.
  - One class rarely reaches the 10-hit floor (2% of calibrated runs at 1 x 30), so the monitor is
    mostly silent until classes pool. The card's "at least 10 hits" condition discloses this.
- `slope()` returns None on singular or non-converging fits; the earlier crash case no longer raises.
- RB12, K0 forward out-of-fold, 223 rows: slope 1.2227. With `slope_interval` I get a 95%
  class-bootstrap interval of 1.06-1.56 at 400 resamples and 1.05-1.56 at 4,000. A 90% interval is
  1.08-1.51. The quoted 1.07-1.52 matches the 90% level, not the 95% the monitor uses.
- Wording: QB6 and WR12 are said to fail, and RB12 and TE6 are said to be hidden as non-charter
  targets, consistently in app.js:13591-13593, app.js:14015, model-card.md:137-140, the ledger-m4
  footer and the registry. `CF_DIR.t0` is gone (app.js:13677). `node --check site/js/app.js` passes.

### Nits (fix when convenient)

1. The RB12 interval "1.07-1.52" at app.js:13592, model-card.md:140 and ledger-m4.md:15 has no
   level, and it is a 90% interval. Either label it "90%" or quote the monitor's 95% interval, 1.05-1.56.
2. The registry kill-condition sentence (m4 maintenance row) is out of order: "...self-check asserts at
   most 10% false kills on calibrated nulls until a refit passes the charter rules". Move "until a refit

## Round 5 (2026-11-25, FF cac8867..7f68e28): TE and QB withdrawals, M5, D2, D5

**Verdict: FAIL.** The TE withdrawal follows the locked rule and the M5 method is mostly sound. The
QB withdrawal does not follow the locked rules, and the same evidence is applied unevenly: WR fails
a test that was used to pull QB, and fails the project's own monitor kill rule, yet stays served.
The registry is stale.

### What I re-derived from committed files

- **M4-oof.csv** (a7379ff) only adds `fwd_BASE` and `loco_BASE`. Every earlier column is identical to
  216775c (max abs diff 0). Every `fwd_BASE` value equals the hit rate of classes `<= c - win`, so the
  base rate is fold-local. In M4.json, every earlier result field is unchanged; `K0_vs_BASE` is added.
- **K0 vs fold-local base rate**, forward, identical rows, `bpm.class_bootstrap` (4,000 resamples,
  seed 7): QB12 98.5%, RB24 100%, WR24 100%, TE12 88.6% (90% gain interval -0.047 to +0.197).
  **TE withdrawal: follows the locked rule** (design M4: at least 90% at every hit line), no tuning.
  The pipeline's 89.4% uses a different gain pooling; both are under 90%.
- **2020+ classes, from M4-oof.csv:** K0 beats the base rate in QB 85.4%, RB 100%, WR 100%, TE 100%
  of resamples. Recalibration slope by era (2013-2019 / 2020+): QB 0.88 / 0.92, RB 0.92 / 0.97,
  WR 0.85 / 1.68, TE 0.66 / 3.17.
- **M5 JSON:** its K0 out-of-fold predictions match M4-oof.csv within 5e-6. Nested slopes: QB 0.68,
  RB 0.95, WR 1.01, TE 0.78. Optimism (nested minus fixed log loss, 90% interval): QB +0.029
  (-0.001 to +0.069), RB -0.007, WR -0.001, TE +0.002. Nested QB still beats the base rate in 90.7%.
- **Self-checks pass** for m4_cut_model, m5 and d2. `node --check site/js/app.js` passes. The frozen
  file serves RB24 and WR24 only, and its `script_sha256` matches the LF-normalized script.
  `pmLadder` is gone. QB and TE cards show the base-rate note (32/158 and 41/203, both correct).
- **D5:** `last_completed_nfl()` derives the last season from the store and asserts it is not past the
  calendar bound. Sound.

### M5 method

Sound in design: the 9-spec grid is fixed, the spec is re-chosen inside each outer fold by inner
leave-one-class-out on training classes only, and the comparison is on identical rows. One confound:
folds with fewer than 3 training classes fall back to an unpenalized linear curve, not a nested
choice, and QB's 2013 fold is one of them. Part of QB's nested 0.68 is that fallback, not selection
optimism. It was not separated out.

### Defects

1. **The QB withdrawal does not follow the locked rules** (7f68e28; model-card.md:169;
   m4_cut_model.py `not_served`).
   - QB passes the locked base-rate bar (98.5%) and the charter slope band (0.89).
   - The design criteria say the 2020+ subset is "reported only, never decisive", yet 2020+ at 85% is
     one of the two stated grounds.
   - The nested slope 0.68 is a point estimate from a non-locked test, partly driven by the linear
     fallback.
   - Withholding a number is the conservative direction, but this is a judgment, not a rule result.
   - Fix: either restore QB and record M5 as a limitation, or label the withdrawal as a judgment
     and apply it evenly (defect 2).
2. **Uneven application: WR stays on stronger adverse evidence.**
   - WR's 2020+ slope is 1.68, with a 90% interval of 1.34-3.19 (M5) and a monitor 95% interval of
     1.30-3.29. Both lie entirely outside 0.75-1.25.
   - `m4_monitor.check` on WR's 2020+ rows returns **kill=True**. On QB's 2020+ rows it returns
     kill=False.
   - Fix: apply one rule to every position. The kill rule the card already commits to would pull WR,
     not QB.
3. **The WR claim is wrong** (model-card.md:173, ledger-m4.md:18, the 7f68e28 message).
   - "Its forward slope of 1.07 holds only on the 2013-2019 classes" is false: the 2013-2019 slope is
     0.85, and 1.07 is the all-folds slope.
   - Fix: "in band on 2013-2019 (0.85), 1.68 on 2020+".
4. **The registry m4 row is stale** (Big Projects master 635cfd9 and agent/m4-registry f3e9fac2).
   - It still says QB and TE top-12 chances are shown and gives QB12 and TE12 slopes as served claims.
   - It omits the TE and QB withdrawals, M5, the ladder removal and the D2 unlinked-draftee note.
   - It says "Rule 6 review passed", which this round does not support.
   - Fix: rewrite the row for RB/WR-only serving and resolve defects 1-3 first.

### Limitations (not defects)

- D2: unlinked draftees get K0 chances with a pick-only note. They are out of population, with 6 hits
  against 9.2 expected (n 80), and too few hits for a slope.
- RB 2013-2019 observed 44 hits against 34.2 expected, outside the 95% count interval (the M7 slice).
  The RB 2020+ classes are calibrated (20 against 21.0).
