# Independent review: population B ship candidates (B8, B9, B10) and the serving path, 2026-10-04

Reviewer: a fresh agent (Opus) that did not build them; reproduced B8/B9/B10 exactly and reverted the
rewritten JSONs so the tree stayed clean.

## Verdict

- **QB B9: REJECT.** It feeds passers five receiving columns (standardized values up to 21.6: D'Juan
  Woods, Ryan Tannehill) that move predictions a lot (Boykin 0.473 with them, 0.188 without). Without
  them QB scores better (0.1087) but its slope is 1.31, failing rule 5; with them the 1.20 slope sits
  inside 0.75-1.25 in only 53% of class resamples (90% interval 0.86-1.65, 20 forward events).
- **RB B10, WR B10, TE B8: CONFIRM, with a display restriction.**

## Findings

1. **Reproduction: PASS.** Every number matches; same sources sha.
2. **Context features (A11/A12 in population B): PASS.** Seasons up to the exit only; same-season
   opponent ratings; joins on (season, player_id, team_id); no NFL information.
3. **B9 nested C: PASS on nesting, REVIEW on stability** (C per forward fold ranges 0.1 to 3.0;
   `chosen_C` mixes forward and leave-one-class-out choices without labels).
4. **QB receiving-feature flaw: FAIL** (above).
5. **Serving path: FAIL as built for classes after the next one.** Backtest of the exact exit-scenario
   transform, trained through 2013 and scoring players as of completed seasons 2014-2017 (5,844
   player-seasons): next class calibrated (mean pd 0.023 vs hit rate 0.026, slope 1.08, AUC 0.909);
   freshmen and sophomores 2.5x too low (0.019 vs 0.045, slope 0.75); freshmen alone worse than a
   constant. The top is not inflated: freshmen and sophomores at pd >= 0.5 hit 7 of 8.
6. **In-progress season counted inconsistently: REVIEW.** `n_seasons` counted the partial season but
   `n_qual` waited for six games, so pd would jump (median 1.18x, up to 1.92x) with no new production.
7. **Displayed exit can disagree with pd's exit: REVIEW.**
8. **Name-prefix link: PASS.** Run alone on all 691 id-known picks it fired once and was right; in the
   full table it links only Ray Rice.
9. **Selection among passing candidates: PASS.** Bias at most 0.0008 (TE, 1.8% of the gain); the WR
   and TE picks are near-ties, which the disclosure should say. B2's feature union also descends from
   v1 pruning that saw every class: a weak effect, disclosed.
