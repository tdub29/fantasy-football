## 1. Deviations from the prereg (recorded, not repaired in place)

- **D1 population provenance.** The prereg says the population cache was built from store 85d9801d. The cache the
  inline run read (sha c79d7ab8) was written 2026-10-05 22:52 PDT from the coordinator's scratch store, ten hours
  before the store the lane copied became 85d9801d. The lane rebuild from its 85d9801d copy (sha cb7ba4c9) has the
  same 2,479 rows and zero mismatches in labels, picks, NFL ids, college links, weights, stars, speed score and
  draft age; only college production features differ, and only H2 reads them. The frozen script rerun on the
  rebuild changed 68 of 344 numbers, all inside H2, and no verdict. Every supplementary number uses the rebuild.
- **D2 occurrence baseline.** The prereg scores the H1 occurrence probability against "an isotonic-on-pick
  occurrence baseline". That is the two-part model's own occurrence part, so the run compared it with a
  per-position base rate fitted on the same embargoed window. S3 keeps that baseline.
- **D3 panel.** The prereg says Love and the frozen 36-player panel are printed; the run printed none. The frozen
  list holds 36 names including Love. Austin Ekeler (2017, undrafted) is not in the population, as in the frozen
  study. S5 prints the rest.
- **D4 ridge alpha.** Alpha was chosen inside the training window, as the prereg says, but by RidgeCV row
  leave-one-out, not by temporal folds. Every row it validates on is mature at decision time, so this is not label
  look-ahead; it lets rows of one class validate a fit that holds the rest of that class. S2 reruns H2 with alpha
  from nested temporal folds.
- **D5 H3 point mapping.** XC0 evaluates the C0 curve at one expected pick. Where a player could go is a spread, and
  C0 is steep near the top, so C0 at the mean pick is not the mean of C0 over the picks (Jensen). S6 adds XC0d.
- **D6 H3 interval.** Three classes resampled with replacement give at most 10 distinct resample means, so the 90%
  interval is coarse. S6 adds a drop-one-class jackknife, descriptive only; no rule reads it.
- **D7 no-link flag.** The H2 design carries a "no college link" flag. 457 of the 1,182 college links run through
  ESPN ids from the nflverse players file, one current snapshot rather than a draft-time one, and the flag's
  relation to outcome changes sign by draft range (audit section 3). Only H2 reads it. Not removed: that would be a new model after the result.
- **D8 roster weight.** DeVonta Smith (2021, pick 10) gets his weight from the draft-year roster, after the
  decision time. One row, H2 only.
- **Withdrawn.** `results.md` notes, post hoc, that H2 lifted the QB Spearman. On the rebuild H2's QB Spearman is
  0.455 against C0's 0.457. The note was feature drift between two store states and is withdrawn in the ledger.

## 2. Protocol for every check below

- Inputs: the lane's store copy 85d9801d (sha asserted before the run, recorded after), the rebuilt population
  cb7ba4c9 (sha asserted against `audit.json`), the lane's copies of the K0 files, the NFLMDD board and the
  nflverse cache, with every network fetch and store ingest disabled. The frozen overall `results.json` (sha
  43f95cdc) is read for comparison and never written. Output: `supplementary.json` next to the prereg.
- The run must reproduce `repro-85d9801d/results.json`: H1 and H2 paired results in all three formats and the F1
  occurrence Brier scores to 1e-12, and H3 to 1e-12 (difference) and 4 decimals (per class). A miss stops the run
  before `supplementary.json` is written.
- Population: drafted QB/RB/WR/TE, classes 2008 to 2023. Scored classes 2012 to 2023, 948 rows. Embargo 3 classes
  (train on classes up to Y-3) everywhere, except the published-rating reference (1 class, all mature classes for
  2024 on), which exists only to reproduce the frozen study's tiers and ratings.
- Uncertainty: class bootstrap, 2000 resamples, seed 7, 90% percentile interval. A window with fewer classes
  resamples its own classes with the same seed.
- Grouping: the draft class is both the fold unit and the resample unit, so teammates in one class share a fold.
  Players related across classes (same college, same NFL depth chart) are not grouped; treating classes as
  independent can understate the intervals.
- Formats: F1 (1QB) primary; F2 (superflex) and F3 (TE premium) are consistency checks. All are 10 teams.
- **K0 format.** The FantasyPros snapshots behind K0 do not record whether the board was 1QB or superflex, so every
  F2 and F3 comparison with K0 is against a board of unknown format.

## 3. Checks (all fixed here; none was run before this file was hashed)

- **S2 nested alpha (H2).** Per outer class Y and position with at least 30 training rows, alpha minimizes summed
  squared residual error (unclipped) over inner folds: each class v in the outer window validates a fit on classes up to v-3,
  with leave-one-class-out C0 targets inside that inner window, and counts only with at least 30 inner training rows
  at the position. Where no inner fold counts (the earliest outer classes), the prereg's row leave-one-out alpha is
  used and the fallback is logged. Read with the prereg rule.
- **S3 calibration.** Occurrence, for the two-part model and the position base rate: Brier, log loss, AUC,
  calibration slope and intercept, calibration in the large. For the two-part model also 10 equal-count
  reliability bins by rank (isotonic outputs tie, so value bins merge), the same metrics by position, and the
  observed rate in its top decile split by position. The Brier ratio (two-part over base rate) with a
  class-bootstrap interval. Magnitude (C0 and H1): deciles, summed prediction over summed outcome with an interval,
  MAE. Descriptive; no rule reads it.
- **S4 windows.** The prereg rule, unchanged, on fixed class windows: 2020 to 2023 (from HOLD = 2020 on,
  `build_prospect_model.py` never used a class to choose a feature), 2012 to 2019, and the 12 drop-one-class
  windows.
- **S5 panel, diagnosis only.** Love, the other frozen panel names and a wider panel fixed now: drafted QB/RB/WR/TE
  from 2024 to 2026 picked 64th or earlier, not already listed. Per player and format: published C0, embargo-3 C0,
  H1 with its occurrence probability, H2; ratings and tiers against the published reference and the embargo-3
  reference, H1 and H2 ratings against their own out-of-sample references; VOR3 where mature, partial VOR through 2025 for 2024
  and 2025 (immature, labelled so), nothing for 2026. Published ratings are compared with the frozen `rating_C0`
  and the largest gap reported. No metric, no tuning; nothing in S5 can change a verdict or a model.
- **S6 H3.** XC0d: for class Y in 2021 to 2023, the mean of the position's C0 curve that scores class Y (fit on
  classes up to Y-3) over the actual picks of the 15 drafted players, any position, from earlier NFLMDD years (2020
  to Y-1) nearest in log board rank; players off the board keep the prereg's point. K = 15 is fixed here, not
  tuned. Also F2 and F3 rows (see K0 format), the jackknife, and the cost of the unknown slot (XC0 or XC0d minus C0
  at the actual pick).
- **T1 tiers.** Tiers use the frozen cuts on a midrank percentile within the 948-row out-of-sample reference, at
  embargo 1 (compared with the frozen tier table; a difference is recorded, not asserted, because that table
  predates the rebuild) and at embargo 3. Per tier: n, share with VOR3 above zero, median, mean, share above p75,
  pick range, by position; and how many rows change tier between the two embargoes, with the crosstab.
- **Why p75 is zero** (known from A1, restated so T1 is read right): 75.4% of drafted players score zero VOR3 in F1
  and F3, so their p75 is exactly 0 and "above p75" means "any positive VOR3"; F2's p75 is 6.25.

## 4. Rules, as coded

- **H1, H2:** FRAGILE if any S4 window, or for H2 the S2 rerun, reaches ACCEPTED under the prereg rule. A move
  between REJECTED and NOT SUPPORTED is reported, not a verdict change.
- **H3:** FRAGILE if XC0d fails the prereg's usable rule in F1. Scope is "F1, F2, F3" if XC0 is usable in F2 and
  F3, else "F1 only".
- When checks disagree, the more conservative reading stands. No 0-100 score is produced or adopted from any of
  this; integration waits for an independent judge.
