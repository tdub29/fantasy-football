How to read a box

Tukey convention: the box spans Q1 to Q3, the heavy rule is the median, whiskers reach the furthest claim within 1.5 × IQR and anything past that is drawn as its own dot. A grouping with fewer than five priced claims gets its raw claims as dots and no box, because a quartile on four numbers is decoration. The axis stops at the furthest whisker, not the furthest claim, because one $200 bid was setting the scale for every chart and squeezing every box into the left fifth of the canvas. A claim past the edge is drawn as a triangle on the edge and that row's max is written out beside its sample size, so the tail is labelled rather than plotted, and the tables carry every exact figure either way. $0 claims are excluded from every box and carried as their own column in the tables: a $0 waiver win is a free-agent pickup that happened to route through the waiver queue, and folding them in drags most medians to zero without telling you what the archetype costs.

What is not in here

Washington Football League is excluded. It runs on ESPN, which is not on api.sleeper.app and exposes no key-free waiver-bid endpoint, so no ESPN claim appears in any number on this page.

Unknown dimensions. Dynasty: experience 0% unknown (2.5% more are team defenses, which have no rookie year and are labelled n/a), position 0%, production profile 20.2%, situation unresolved 0%. Redraft: experience 0% (16.6% team defenses), position 0%, production 12.6%, situation 0%. The production profile needs three games after the pickup, so week-16-and-later claims and the two weeks of 2026 played so far are unknown rather than guessed.

Losing bids. Failed claims carry a real stated price but several rosters bid on the same player in the same week, so charting them would count one vacancy many times. They stay in notes/faab-bids-2026-09-16.csv under status for anyone who wants them.
