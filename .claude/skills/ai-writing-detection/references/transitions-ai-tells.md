# Transitions to avoid (AI tells)

Companion to [ai-writing-detection.md](ai-writing-detection.md). Extracted from copywriting transition guidance; use for a quick scan of signpost phrases that often read as AI-generated.

## Concluding copy

Avoid **"In conclusion"** at the start of a paragraph. It is overused and signals AI writing.

Prefer natural wrap-ups such as: wrapping up, the bottom line is, here's what to do next, final thoughts, now it's your turn.

## Phrases to avoid

These are overused in AI-generated content:

- "That being said,..."
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "At its core,..."
- "In today's digital landscape,..."
- "When it comes to the realm of..."
- "This begs the question..."
- "Let's delve into..."

For the full list (openers, transitions, closers, structure), see [ai-writing-detection.md](ai-writing-detection.md).
