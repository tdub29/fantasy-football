# Ariely: Cash Distance

## When to Use

Use when value is one or more steps removed from cash:

- Points, rewards, credits, miles, gift cards.
- Reimbursement balances.
- Store credits or promotional credits.
- Internal tokens or cash-equivalent perks.

## Framework

People treat cash as more morally real than cash-equivalents. Each step away from cash makes misuse easier to rationalize and spending easier to ignore.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the medium.**
2. **Map distance from cash.** Cash, card, credit, points, token, item.
3. **Dollarize the value.**
4. **Identify rationalization risk.**
5. **Add visibility.**
6. **Design controls or reminders.**

## Common Failure Modes

- **Points feel free.** Users overspend because dollars are hidden.
- **Credits evade scrutiny.** Non-cash balances bypass budget discipline.
- **Policy abuse.** Taking objects or credits feels less like taking money.
- **No dollar equivalent.** Users cannot compare alternatives.
