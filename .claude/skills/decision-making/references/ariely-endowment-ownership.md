# Ariely: Endowment Ownership

## When to Use

Use when ownership changes perceived value:

- Free premium trials.
- Cancellation or downgrade flows.
- Connected account and data export UX.
- SMB seller valuation.
- User-created budgets/categories/rules.

## Framework

Once people feel ownership, giving something up feels like a loss. Owners value the thing more than buyers do, and virtual ownership can start before legal ownership.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the owned thing.**
2. **Identify attachment source.** Time, customization, trial access, labor, identity, sunk setup.
3. **Compare owner vs buyer value.**
4. **Flag loss framing.**
5. **Check ethical risk.** Is attachment being used to trap the user?
6. **Design fair transition.** Export, downgrade, pause, refund, or cooling-off.

## Common Failure Modes

- **Trial trap.** Users feel they own premium features before deciding.
- **Customization lock-in.** Setup labor becomes a switching barrier.
- **Seller overvaluation.** The owner prices memories, not market value.
- **Loss-only copy.** Downgrade/cancel language exaggerates loss.
