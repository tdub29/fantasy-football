# Galef: Escape Your Echo Chamber

## When to Use

Use when the user needs better dissent:

- A thesis has only friendly sources.
- Bear cases are strawmen or rage-bait.
- The user wants credible disagreement.
- A product/SMB decision needs opposing evidence from people with shared goals.

## The Framework

Galef does not say "listen to anyone who disagrees." She recommends seeking people you find reasonable, people with common ground, and people who share goals but disagree on means.

## How to Apply

1. **State the belief to challenge.**
2. **Define reasonable dissent criteria.** Expertise, honesty, shared goals, evidence quality.
3. **Map dissent sources.** People, communities, reports, competitors, customers, operators.
4. **Filter out bad dissent.** Rage-bait, tribal enemies, weak strawmen.
5. **Extract strongest objections.**
6. **Identify common ground.**
7. **State update implications.** What would change if the dissent is right?

## Output Format

```markdown
## Echo Chamber Escape: {belief}

Belief:
Reasonable dissent criteria:

| Source | Why reasonable | Common ground | Strongest objection |
|--------|----------------|---------------|---------------------|

Weak dissent ignored:
Update implications:
Next source to read/contact:
```

## Common Failure Modes

- **Hate-reading.** Choosing unreasonable opponents to feel correct.
- **Token dissent.** One weak bear case satisfies the checkbox.
- **No shared goals.** Disagreement is too far from the user's actual decision.
- **Source hoarding.** Reading dissent without extracting update implications.
