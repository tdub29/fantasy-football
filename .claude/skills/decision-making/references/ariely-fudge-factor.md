# Ariely: Fudge Factor

## When to Use

Use when small dishonesty is easy to rationalize:

- Expense or reimbursement claims.
- Referral program abuse.
- Self-reported financial information.
- Policy bypasses.
- Manual categorization or tagging.

## Framework

People often cheat a little while preserving a self-image of honesty. Big crimes are easier to condemn; small gray-zone violations need reminders, salience, and controls.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the honesty-sensitive action.**
2. **Identify rationalization path.**
3. **Estimate monitoring gap.**
4. **Add moral reminder.** Signature, pledge, code, or prompt before action.
5. **Reduce ambiguity.**
6. **Add proportional control.**

## Common Failure Modes

- **Only policing big fraud.** Small cheats compound.
- **Reminder after action.** Moral reminders work best before the choice.
- **Ambiguous policy.** Vague rules invite self-serving interpretation.
- **Over-surveillance.** Heavy controls can damage trust.
