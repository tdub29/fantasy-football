# Ariely: Expectations Framing

## When to Use

Use when copy or context changes the experience itself:

- Onboarding promises.
- Premium report naming.
- Feature previews.
- Financial confidence copy.
- SMB/customer offer framing.

## Framework

Expectations shape perception and performance. Labels, brand cues, presentation, and identity primes can change what users experience, not just what they report.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the experience.**
2. **List expectation cues.** Name, copy, price, brand, visuals, ritual, order.
3. **Check pre-disclosure effects.** Does revealing a flaw before use change perception?
4. **Identify identity primes.**
5. **Separate truthful framing from manipulation.**
6. **Rewrite cues to set accurate expectations.**

## Common Failure Modes

- **Overpromising.** Expectation exceeds delivery and creates disappointment.
- **Negative pre-disclosure.** A minor flaw becomes salient too early.
- **Identity priming harm.** Copy makes users feel less capable.
- **Placebo confusion.** Price effects belong in `ariely-price-placebo`.
