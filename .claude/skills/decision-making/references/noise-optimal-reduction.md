# Noise: Optimal Reduction

## When to Use

Use when deciding how much process to add:

- Full MAP for SMB deals vs lightweight screen.
- Noise audit for budget categories.
- Automated support triage.
- Multi-reviewer UX gates.
- Portfolio review protocols.

## Framework

Noise reduction has costs: time, morale, rigidity, automation risk, and lost flexibility. The goal is not zero noise everywhere; it is the right level of noise for the stakes.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the judgment.**
2. **Estimate stakes.** Financial, user harm, reversibility.
3. **Estimate frequency.**
4. **Estimate current noise.**
5. **Estimate reduction cost.** Time, tooling, friction, morale.
6. **Check dignity/flexibility.** Will users or reviewers feel like cogs?
7. **Choose structure level.** None, checklist, guideline, audit, MAP, rule, algorithm.

## Common Failure Modes

- **Zero-noise fantasy.** Chasing consistency where diversity is valuable.
- **Process theater.** Adding ceremony without measuring disagreement.
- **Under-structuring high stakes.** Expensive judgments stay informal.
- **Ignoring dignity.** People reject systems that erase legitimate judgment.
