# Galef: Scout Behavior Audit

## When to Use

Use when the user wants evidence of their actual reasoning habits:

- Before a major portfolio or product decision.
- After a stock review to audit process quality.
- When the user claims to be objective or open-minded.
- When criticism is being avoided or handled poorly.

## The Framework

Galef's signs of a scout are behavioral, not self-image. The audit requires concrete examples of truth-seeking behavior, not declarations of rationality.

## What to Check

- **Credit others when they are right.**
- **Track how you handle criticism.**
- **Find examples where you proved yourself wrong.**
- **Use anti-fooling precautions, such as blind analysis or prewritten criteria.**
- **Seek good critics, not just friendly critics.**

## How to Apply

1. **Pick the decision domain.**
2. **Collect concrete incidents.** No abstractions; use actual examples.
3. **Score each scout sign.** Strong, mixed, weak.
4. **Identify anti-fooling gaps.**
5. **Name useful critics.**
6. **Choose one process upgrade before the next decision.**

## Output Format

```markdown
## Scout Behavior Audit: {domain}

Concrete examples:
Credit-others score:
Criticism score:
Proved-wrong examples:
Anti-fooling precautions:
Good critics:
Process upgrade:
```

## Common Failure Modes

- **Virtue self-rating.** "I'm open-minded" without incidents.
- **Friendly critics only.** The critic pool never challenges the real thesis.
- **No blind safeguards.** Criteria are rewritten after seeing the answer.
- **Outcome substitute.** Good results are used as evidence of good scout behavior.
