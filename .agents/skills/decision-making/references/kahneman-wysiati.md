# Kahneman — WYSIATI (What You See Is All There Is)

## The framework

> "WYSIATI, which stands for what you see is all there is." (Ch 7, lines 3139–3140)

System 1's signature failure: it constructs a coherent story from whatever evidence is at hand and feels certain about it, completely insensitive to what evidence is MISSING. Worse: confidence in the story tracks coherence, not completeness.

> "The confidence that individuals have in their beliefs depends mostly on the quality of the story they can tell about what they see, even if they see little." (lines 3197–3199)

### Three things WYSIATI produces

**1. Overconfidence**
You feel sure because the story is coherent — not because the story is complete. A 30-second briefing can produce 90% confidence.

**2. Framing effects**
"90% survival rate" and "10% mortality rate" are the same data. WYSIATI means you respond to what's in front of you, not what's been omitted. See references/kahneman-framing-effects.md.

**3. Base-rate neglect**
You see Steve described as "shy and orderly" and conclude he's a librarian — ignoring the fact that there are 20× more farmers than librarians. The base rate isn't IN the description, so it isn't IN your judgment. See references/kahneman-representativeness-base-rate.md.

### The mechanism

> "System 1 excels at constructing the best possible story that incorporates ideas currently activated, but it does not (cannot) allow for information it does not have." (lines 3104–3110)

System 1 doesn't have a "missing data" alarm. It works with what's present and builds confidence proportional to coherence, not coverage.

## How to apply

When forming a confident judgment:

1. **Ask: what would I need to know to be sure?** Make a list. How much of that list do you actually have?
2. **Ask: who's NOT represented in the evidence?** Whose story am I not hearing? What outcomes have been filtered out (survivorship bias)?
3. **Specifically check base rates.** Even when the case description is vivid, the underlying frequency matters.
4. **For high-stakes decisions: actively seek disconfirming information.** Default mode is to consume confirming. Force the opposite.
5. **Distinguish coherence from completeness.** A story can be perfectly coherent and built on 5% of the relevant evidence.

## Common failure modes

- **"But it all fits!"** Coherence is what System 1 manufactures from any evidence. The fit doesn't validate the story.
- **Asking for "more information" but only checking if the new info confirms.** WYSIATI persists if your search is biased.
- **Confusing certainty (subjective feeling) with accuracy (objective track record).** They have low correlation in low-validity environments.
