# Anti-slop design law

The full law lives at [`references/slop-law.md`](references/slop-law.md) (source: https://pols.dev/slop.md). It is the authority on what AI design slop is and how to avoid it — generic look-alike output, em dashes in copy, glowy pill buttons, oversized-icon-in-a-tile, default Fraunces+Work Sans pairings, cut-off glows, floating decorative cards, and the rest of the named tells.

## How to use

1. **Read `references/slop-law.md` in full before you design or build any interface**, and again before you ship — walk it point by point and fix what falls short.
2. **The user's explicit direction always wins.** Everything in the law is the DEFAULT; if the user asked for a specific color, layout, or effect the law warns against, do exactly what they asked.
3. **De-slop requests** ("de-slop this", "remove the AI tells"): audit the target against the law and fix each violation in place.

## Relationship to other skills

- Complements `impeccable` (UX/hierarchy/states), `hallmark` (macrostructure + genre + anti-slop gates), and `de-slop` / `design-taste-frontend` (the anti-slop pass). When those run on a design task, this law is the shared taste bar.
- On projects with a locked design system (a `DESIGN.md` / `design.md`), that system overrides these defaults for that project; apply the law only where it does not conflict with the locked system.
