These are the **initial rankings** for the 2026 cycle, organized under Domain's player category system. Use as the baseline before slotting in further 2026 rookies.

## Player Category System

- **Cornerstone** — Highly insulated asset with proven elite production. Ceiling already realized but room to grow due to elite nature. Value unlikely to dip after a bad season.
- **Foundational** — Tier below cornerstone; elite upside but with notable questions (situation, sample size, age, or consistency). Includes a "depreciating pillars" sub-group: still elite production but priced where they're nearly guaranteed to lose year-over-year value (Cook, JT, Hurts, Nico).
- **Upside Premier** — Unproven talents (mostly rookies / 2nd-year players) who haven't earned the foundational nod yet but plausibly could this year. Ranked by likelihood of moving up to Foundational.
- **Mainstay / Productive Vet / Short-Term League Winner** — Lower tiers (not covered in this video).
